# Use sqlite for database

## Context and Problem Statement

How do we store our data?
How do we retrieve our data?
How do we ensure smooth querying?

## Considered Options

* Sqlite
* Sled
* Nosql

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Sqlite", because it fits both the future vision of advanced queries, and as a industry staple it is well documented
